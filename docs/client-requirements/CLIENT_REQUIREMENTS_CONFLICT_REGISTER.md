- Missing Employee documents, Leave, KPI, communication center and CRM consent are gaps, not conflicts.
- Gift Voucher/Customer Gold Purchase exclusion from invoice search is an implementation gap, not a client-internal conflict.
- Barcode extra master codes `ROS`/`CSD` in the current DB are current-source scope differences; they are not silently treated as client requirements.
