# DARFUS UX-1 Design Philosophy

`DARFUS OBSIDIAN ATELIER` is the working direction: modern jewellery operations, gold/financial precision, contemporary enterprise UX and controlled safe motion.

Premium means restraint, material precision and clear hierarchy. Gold is identity/selection/key-value emphasis, never wallpaper. The system must remain fast for daily ERP use, data-dense where the task requires it, Arabic/English correct, and usable in dark and light modes. The invariant is `DESIGN MAY CHANGE; SYSTEM BEHAVIOR MUST NOT CHANGE`.

The three prototypes are static, read-only reference surfaces. They do not define new workflows, fields, calculations, permissions, API payloads or business state.
