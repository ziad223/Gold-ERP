# UX-11 Business Document Contract Freeze

The following are explicitly frozen for this control:

- Invoice, receipt, Gift Voucher, reservation receipt, and CGP business identities.
- Invoice numbers, dates, customers, employee attribution, totals, tax, payment, and status.
- Asset ID, active barcode, QR/barcode payload, RFID display, and lifecycle state.
- Inventory, accounting, journal, payable, payment, VAT, and tax snapshot semantics.
- Server print/reprint authorization, audit events, permissions, and routes.
- Gift Voucher state/financial mapping and CGP repeated-print recovery behavior.

Only presentation classes, preview containment, focus-visible affordances, and print color isolation may change.
