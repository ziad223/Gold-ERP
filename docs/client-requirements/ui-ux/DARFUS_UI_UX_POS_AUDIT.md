# POS Audit

Real browser proof covered AR/EN and dark mode. POS hierarchy is Customer → Product Search → Invoice Items → Payment & Totals; customer, branch, cashier, payment modes, Gift Voucher panel, tax, total and checkout action are visible. The dark screenshot shows a cohesive three-column workspace and a disabled checkout when the cart is empty.

Strengths: clear operational sections, branch/cashier context, asset-oriented search, explicit payment modes, total emphasis, and fail-closed empty cart. Risks: dense desktop composition, long customer panel, mixed AR payment labels (`Cash`, `Card`, `Transfer`), and two controls without names in the accessibility DOM sample. No sale was executed.
