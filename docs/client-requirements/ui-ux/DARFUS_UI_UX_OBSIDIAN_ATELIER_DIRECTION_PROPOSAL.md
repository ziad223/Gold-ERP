# DARFUS OBSIDIAN ATELIER — Refined Direction Proposal

## Philosophy

Modern jewellery atelier identity + precision gold/financial terminal + enterprise ERP discipline. Premium through restraint and material cues, not decoration.

## System direction

- Dark: deep obsidian/navy surfaces, warm off-white foreground, restrained gold for priority/rates/selected states, teal or semantic colors for operational states.
- Light: warm-neutral background, clear white/elevated surfaces, charcoal foreground, the same restrained gold and semantic status system.
- Gold policy: never every button, every border, or every card; never wallpaper.
- Typography: Cairo/appropriate Arabic face for Arabic chrome; Inter/appropriate Latin face for English; preserve business data language. Numeric islands use tabular numerals and LTR isolation.
- Spacing/surfaces: compact but breathable operational rhythm; shallow radius and measured elevation; hierarchy by spacing and surface, not repeated cards.
- Navigation: stable global shell, responsive collapse, clear active state, no second workflow.
- Tables: primary identity first, priority columns, responsive details/list on narrow devices, numeric alignment and visible totals.
- Forms: clear sections, required/optional hierarchy, inline errors, contextual help, no invented fields or wizard conversion.
- POS: preserve current sequence; optimize search, identity, totals, payment reachability and recovery.
- Financial: legible base/VAT/total and debit/credit/balance; no visual treatment that obscures numbers.
- Responsive: seven-class evidence gate; prioritize content and action, do not only shrink desktop.
- Motion: safe transform/opacity only; no decorative infinite motion; reduce/disable for critical operations and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Accessibility: labels, names, focus, touch, contrast, errors, dialog focus, table semantics, reduced motion.
- Language: AR chrome Arabic-first; EN chrome English-only; business data and canonical technical terms preserved.

Explicit rejects: generic admin template, traditional ERP with gold paint, black/gold casino, glow that reduces readability, and luxury decoration that harms scanning.
